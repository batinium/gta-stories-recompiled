GTA Stories Recompiled
Liberty City Stories and Vice City Stories, recompiled for PC and Android — and joined into one world on PC: drive across the bridge from Portland to Vice City, play as Vic or Toni, and take your cars both ways.

Trailer: https://youtu.be/OvINqoUC0Uo

- Bring your own discs: nothing from the games is included. The launcher builds everything from your discs (PC); the Android app builds or imports a game library made from your disc.
- Includes a mod loader for both games.
- Android: runtime-only app, both games; building on the phone is experimental.
- Built on gothamor's gta-vcs-recomp (Vice City Stories) and PS2Recomp. Unofficial fan project, not affiliated with Rockstar or Take-Two.

Downloads: GTA-Stories-Recompiled-0.3.0-windows.zip (PC), the APK (Android, sha256 below). See the README for instructions.
