GTA Stories Recompiled 0.3.1
A small fix release for Android, and the macOS build.

- Android: dialogs keep their buttons on short screens. On a phone held sideways (or a folded phone) the "Build" button of "Build code pack on this phone" could end up below the screen's edge, so the build could not be started.
- Android: crash reports without a PC. The game now keeps its log on the phone, and About > "Game log and crash report…" saves it to a file or shares it, with the phone's model, chip and Android version. If the game crashes on your phone, please send that report with your issue.
- macOS: the launcher as a disk image (GTA-Stories-Recompiled-0.3.1-macos-arm64.dmg): open it and drag the app to Applications. Apple silicon, macOS 13 or later.
- Windows: no changes; use the 0.3.0 zip.

Bring your own discs: nothing from the games is included. Unofficial fan project, not affiliated with Rockstar or Take-Two.
